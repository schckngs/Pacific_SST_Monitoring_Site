Buoy temperature in the Canadian Pacific Exclusive Economic Zone and Northeast Pacific

Authors

Andrea Hilborn

Charles Hannah

Published

October 19, 2026

When using sea surface temperature datasets to monitor marine heatwaves (MHWs), it is important to compare them to other data sources. Here, we assembled temperatures measured by buoys located in the Canadian Pacific EEZ, as well as further throughout the Northeast Pacific (NEP).

Canadian Pacific Exclusive Economic Zone buoy temperatures

ECCC buoy data

  • Dataset: Realtime data from Environment and Climate Change Canada buoys
  • Data access: CIOOS Pacific ERDDAP DFO_MEDS_BUOYS and ECCC_MSC_BUOYS records. The DFO_MEDS_BUOYS record has a detailed quality control layer outlined in Kellogg et al. (2021) but ceased in December 2023
  • Climatology period: 1991–2020 (where available)

Northeast Pacific buoy temperatures

In addition to the ECCC buoy data, NOAA’s National Buoy Data Center reports temperature data from buoys, with many time series dating back more than 30 years. Data from NDBC buoys have been added below, so that we can view current SSTs and SST anomalies throughout the Northeast Pacific ocean.

NDBC buoy data

  • Dataset: NDBC Standard Meteorological Buoy Data
  • Data access: NOAA Coastwatch West Coast Regional Node ERDDAP cwwcNDBCMet record
  • Climatology period: 1991–2020 (where available)
